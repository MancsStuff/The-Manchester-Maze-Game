The Manchester Maze — An Interactive Memoir
A 5-minute interactive memoir based on the book The Manchester Maze by Christopher Ryan Leece.
Limeside Estate, 1985. You're a kid. No one knows you're different. Not even you.
Walk through key moments from working-class Manchester in the 80s and 90s — the nursery, the alley, the corner shop, the kitchen at 19 with your dad's £20 on the table. Three different endings depending on the choices you make.

About the book
The Manchester Maze is a memoir about growing up with undiagnosed autism and ADHD on a Manchester council estate, and the long way back from there. It covers the 1980s, gangs and rave culture, escape to Kent, a late diagnosis in Spain, and the slow rebuild after.
Buy the book on Amazon: https://amzn.eu/d/0iXJTKHE
About the game
Built in a single HTML file. No build step, no framework install, no database — just open it in a browser. Works on phones, tablets and desktops.
Tech: React 18 + Three.js + Babel standalone, all from CDN
3D walking transitions between scenes, rendered live in the browser
Cinematic intro using stills from the book's promotional video
Real photos of the actual locations from the memoir as scene backgrounds
Three endings driven by your accumulated choices, not just the last one
Content note
Contains references to violence, drug use, bullying and self-harm. Names, places and events have been fictionalised.
